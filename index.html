01 / Profile

The short version

Originally from Manchester, England, I moved to the US at 15 and never lost the habit of adapting fast. I am especially drawn to credit, investing, and financial analysis, and most of that pull came from getting my hands dirty rather than from a textbook.

At The First Bank of Greenwich I sat in commercial credit, pulling apart borrower financials, sizing repayment capacity, and running CRE metrics like NOI, DSCR, and LTV. At 3i Group in London I worked with the infrastructure team, building investment profiles and presenting findings to management. Before that, I ran my own resale business, which taught me more about pricing and markets than most classes did.
